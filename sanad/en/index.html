Frequently asked questions

Does Sanad reply in Arabic and English?

Yes. It replies in the customer's language, in a tone set for your business.

Will Sanad send anything without my knowledge?

It only auto-answers the routine questions you approved. Anything with a special price or commitment reaches you first for one-tap approval.

What if Sanad does not know the answer?

It apologises politely and hands the conversation to you at once with a summary of the request.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

A dedicated WhatsApp Business number is recommended. If you want to keep your current one, we review the options with you in the discovery session.

How long does setup take?

Three to five working days after the discovery session and receiving the required details.

Where is my customer data stored?

In your own accounts, such as Google Sheets and your mailbox. Details are in the privacy policy.
