      Frequently asked questions

      Why are Munjiz prices lower?

      Because we submit requests online, we save on trips and time and pass the saving on to you. If the authority needs you in person for a medical exam or fingerprinting, we let you know the appointment and details in advance.

      Does the price include government fees?

      The price shown is the Munjiz fee. Government fees are paid as set by the authority, and we send you the official receipt.

      What does "In packages" mean?

      Services marked this way are deducted from the monthly package balance, each counting as one service. Other services and bundles are paid separately with the package discount.

      How do I get a quote?

      Fill in the request form and press "Send on WhatsApp" or "Send by email". We reply with the price and the documents needed before starting.

      Is approval guaranteed?

      Approval and timing rest with the government authority. What we commit to is submitting your request accurately, following it up and keeping you informed of the result.

      How do I pay?

      Service fees are paid once agreed, before we submit the request; subscriptions are paid in advance at the start of each period.
