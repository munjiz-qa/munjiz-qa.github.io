      Frequently asked questions

      Why are Munjiz prices lower?

      We submit online with no in-person visits or trips; you attend the medical exam or fingerprinting yourself if the authority requires it. That keeps your cost down.

      Does the price include government fees?

      No. The price is the Munjiz fee only. Government fees are paid as charged and we send you the official receipt.

      What does "In packages" mean?

      Services marked this way are deducted from the monthly package balance, each counting as one service. Other services and bundles are paid separately with the package discount.

      How do I get a quote?

      Fill in the request form and press "Send on WhatsApp" or "Send by email". We reply with the price and the documents needed before starting.

      Do you guarantee approval or timing?

      The authority's decision and timing are outside our guarantee. We guarantee an accurate submission and that we tell you the result.

      How do I pay?

      Single services on agreement before submission; subscriptions in advance.
