Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · See also the data and confidentiality section of the legal documents.

Munjiz Business Services LLC respects its clients' privacy and processes personal data under Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law No. 13 of 2016. This page explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Sign-up and contact details: name, mobile number, email and company name if provided.
  • Transaction details: the services requested, and your QID or commercial registration number when needed to complete the transaction.
  • Copies of documents you send us to submit the transaction.
  • Our contact history with you, your transaction status and your document expiry dates.

Your sign-up details are kept in your own browser so you do not need to re-enter them; they reach us when you sign up, send a request or create your account, and are then kept in Munjiz's private records. The site uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

Why we use it

  • To review your request and send you the documents list and the price.
  • To submit and follow your transaction with the competent authority on your authorisation.
  • To update you on its status and remind you of renewal dates.
  • To issue invoices and meet our accounting and legal obligations.

We do not sell your data or use it for anything unrelated to your service.

Who we share it with

  • The government authority your transaction is submitted to, only as far as it needs.
  • Licensed partners (translation offices) when you request a service they provide, after telling you.
  • Technical providers that host our data (Google), under their data protection commitments.

Retention

We keep transaction data while you work with us and then for the period required by accounting and tax rules. We delete document copies within 90 days of closing the transaction unless you ask us to keep them for later renewals.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent to be contacted, and we will reply within 30 days. Contact us on WhatsApp +974 51600498 or by email at munijizqa@gmail.com.
