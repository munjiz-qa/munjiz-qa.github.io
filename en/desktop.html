Munjiz on your computer

Run Munjiz from your own computer as a standalone app in its own window. It opens from your desktop in one click and needs no outside tool or service.

Easiest · one minute

1. Install Munjiz as an app

Works on Windows, Mac and Linux with Chrome or Edge, and with Safari on Mac. Munjiz appears with its icon in your programs and on the desktop, and opens in its own window.

Munjiz is installed and running as an app on this device.

  1. Open the admin panel in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon ⊕ at the end of the address bar, then Install.
  3. In Safari on Mac: File menu, then Add to Dock.

Clients can do the same from My account. Pages opened once stay readable when the internet is down.

Fully standalone · offline

2. Munjiz desktop package

A complete copy of the website and admin panel that runs from your computer and can work without internet. The package updates automatically with every change to the site.

⬇ Download the package (ZIP)
  1. Unzip the file to a permanent place, such as Documents.
  2. Windows: double-click Start-Munjiz-Windows.bat. If Windows protection appears, choose "More info" then "Run anyway".
  3. Mac: right-click Start-Munjiz-Mac.command and choose "Open".
  4. Linux: run Start-Munjiz-Linux.sh.

Munjiz opens in your browser at http://localhost:8765. Windows needs no extra software.

Where is your data saved?

Connected to the office server: all data is on the central Munjiz server, so it is the same on your computer, your phone and the published site, from any device.

Offline on this computer: data is in this computer's browser only. Do not clear browser data, take a backup at least weekly, and restore it from the same section.

First admin sign-in in offline mode: username 123 and password 123; the panel asks you to change both at once. In connected mode, use your usual sign-in.
