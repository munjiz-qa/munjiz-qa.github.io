البيانات التي تبقى على جهازك

يحفظ التطبيق في ذاكرة المتصفح على جهازك فقط: الإعدادات (اللغة والخط والوضع الليلي وطريقة حساب مواقيت الصلاة)، والعلامات المرجعية، وتقدم الحفظ والتسميع وعدّاد الأذكار، والأبحاث التي تحفظها في «أبحاثي»، وحساب الدخول الاختياري. لا يُرسَل شيء من ذلك إلى أي خادم، وكلمات المرور تُحفظ مشفرة بخوارزمية PBKDF2 ولا تُحفظ نصاً صريحاً. يمكنك محو هذه البيانات كلها في أي وقت بمسح بيانات التطبيق أو بيانات الموقع من إعدادات الجهاز أو المتصفح، أو بحذف التطبيق.

الموقع الجغرافي

يُطلب الموقع فقط عندما تضغط بنفسك زر «تحديد موقعي» في قسم مواقيت الصلاة، ويُستعمل على جهازك لحساب المواقيت واتجاه القبلة، ولا يُرسَل إلى أي جهة. يمكنك بدلاً من ذلك اختيار الدولة والمدينة يدوياً دون منح الإذن.

الأذونات على أندرويد

يطلب تطبيق أندرويد إذن الإنترنت لتحميل الموقع وتحديثه، وإذن الموقع الجغرافي فقط عند ضغط زر «تحديد موقعي». لا يطلب الوصول إلى الصور أو جهات الاتصال أو الميكروفون أو الكاميرا.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Applies to the Mishkat website and its Android, Windows, iOS and desktop apps.

In short: Mishkat never asks for your name, email or phone number, shows no ads, uses no tracking or analytics tools, and never sells or shares any data. Everything you save stays on your device.

Data kept on your device

The app stores only in your device's browser storage: settings (language, font, dark mode, prayer-time method), bookmarks, memorization and review progress, the adhkar counter, papers you save in "My research", and the optional sign-in account. None of it is sent to any server, and passwords are stored as PBKDF2 hashes, never as plain text. You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's or the site's data in your device or browser settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Location

Location is requested only when you tap "Locate me" in the prayer-times section. It is used on your device to compute prayer times and the qibla direction and is never sent anywhere. You can pick your country and city by hand instead, without granting the permission.

Optional requests to outside services

Most of the app works offline. It contacts an outside service only when you use a feature that needs one:

FeatureServiceWhat is sent
Listening to a recitationeveryayah.comA request for the verse's audio file only, as any browser does when it opens an audio file.
Machine translation of the interface into a language that is not built inGoogle TranslateOnly the general interface text to translate. None of your data is sent with it.
Links that search YouTube for a storyYouTubeNothing, unless you open the link yourself; it then opens in your browser.

These services follow their own privacy policies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log ordinary technical connection data such as IP addresses for security and operation.

Android permissions

The Android app uses the Internet permission to load and update the site, and asks for location only when you tap "Locate me". It does not access your photos, contacts, microphone or camera.

Children

Mishkat's content, including the children's corner, is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) with a strict Content Security Policy that blocks scripts from outside the site.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page with its effective date. For privacy questions, write to the developer at the email address shown on the app's page in the store you downloaded it from.
